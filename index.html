BEYOND THE RESUME

I'm a Systems Engineering student at Texas A&M International University with a passion for creativity, technology, and turning ideas into something meaningful.

As a first-generation Latina in engineering, I've learned that innovation isn't just about technical knowledge. It's also about curiosity, collaboration, and being willing to think differently. Whether I'm coding, working on robotics, or exploring design, I enjoy challenging myself and discovering new ways to solve problems.

Through this portfolio, I hope to share what I'm building, what I'm learning, and the experiences shaping my journey. More importantly, I hope to encourage others, especially women and younger students, to explore STEM and pursue ideas of their own.

Engineering isn't just about what we create, but also about the people we inspire along the way.
